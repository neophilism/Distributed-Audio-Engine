# TrackZero

TrackZero is consumer-facing linear radio, named for hidden pre-track-one bonus recordings. Artists or organizations program music, news, documentary or other shows through Distributed Radio Engine. The listener joins the current stream; no track picker, per-user audio insertion or location/proximity requirement is added.

## One contribution metric per creator

The displayed metric is **artist points** (publisher points for nonmusic). Points measure verified out-loud contribution, not a claim about a measured audience. Sound level is logarithmic: do not add decibel readings as though they were energy. For each distinct verified Bluetooth-speaker output, integrate reference-level acoustic-energy equivalents over eligible minutes:

`points = Σ eligible_minutes × 10^((conservative_measured_dBA − reference_dBA)/10)`

Conservative level is measured level minus calibrated uncertainty. Require that it exceeds the creator's disclosed threshold and that measured level plus uncertainty remains within the governing safety ceiling. Reference measurement geometry, A weighting, averaging window, calibration, policy version and fixed-point rounding are pinned. Threshold/default units require a supported-device field pilot; illustrative numbers never become automatic loudness recommendations.

Verified louder outputs and independently verified additional speakers contribute more. Declared wattage, phone volume percentage, paired-but-unused outputs, headphones, mute, stale evidence and unverified multi-speaker counts contribute zero. An aggregate field observation and its individual outputs cannot both be counted. Duplicate physical outputs across phones cannot multiply credit. Measurement does not establish that bystanders heard the program and requires no precise location.

The UI calculates provisional progress in real time. Spendable points vest once per complete broadcast occurrence only after the song/show and its closing identification finish. For music that is `That was SONG by ARTIST on STATION radio.` Stop/pause/seek/mute or playback interruption before closure forfeits that occurrence's provisional points, including an interruption during its announcement. Late joining starts point-cycle eligibility at the next full item. Unverified acoustic windows earn zero while otherwise continuous ordinary playback remains available. Ads/interstitials do not earn song points; no point credit for merely following or headphone listening.

Each item explicitly names its beneficiary creator and any agreed shares. Multi-artist stations credit the relevant artist wallet rather than the station operator by default. Shares conserve total credit. Repeat aired occurrences can earn again; replaying the same occurrence/evidence cannot. Artist wallets cannot be traded or combined into a universal pool. Lifetime earned and spendable balance are views of the same metric; spending never resets listener history or rarity eligibility.

## Spending and long-tail prices

Creators set any legitimate point prices. The default tier cost is `ceil(base × (1 + gentle_growth × tier) × exp(acceleration × tier²))` with bounded configuration and arbitrary-precision accounting. Initial changes are small and high-tier costs grow exponentially; there is no fixed ultimate maximum reward tier. Publish 1000-speaker, all-day simulations to tune challenge without incentivizing out-of-policy output. Offer versions snapshot price, availability and terms; later edits cannot erase earned balances or granted recordings. Atomic redemption spends the chosen artist's points and grants that artist's reward; stock/budget failures do not debit points.

## Unlocked Grooves: separate free releases

Artists set aside app-exclusive recordings, including alternate takes, in a catalog separate from paid and points rewards. A final cut and a different take are distinct recordings. The same recording is not simultaneously a Groove and points/paid offer under this specification.

Broadcasters set a common opportunity cadence every x-to-y completed items. The opportunity is always at the shared instant after the prior closing identification and before the next opening identification. Every connected listener observes the same opportunity ID/time; personal eligibility can yield different bonus grants without changing the audio sequence. Headphones and speakers both qualify through completed listening. Speaker points are never required for a Groove.

Rarity weights decrease exponentially with tier, with an explicit positive tail and creator-controlled lifetime completed-listen gates. Superlisteners become eligible for the rarest takes and have more opportunities; listening longer does not drive their entire chance of future rewards to zero. Random draws are generated by the authorized issuer with auditable commitments, not client clocks. Persist completion counts and one result per listener/opportunity across reconnects; do not reset or reroll at login or route change. Explain cadence, rarity and eligibility without implying a guaranteed rare grant.

On a successful grant, show **LOCKED GROOVE**, animate **LOCKED** into **UNLOCKED**, then present the bonus. Reduced-motion and screen-reader paths display UNLOCKED GROOVE directly with the same entitlement. The reveal does not stop radio, insert a personalized audio track or let the listener choose the next song. Downloads and acquired rights appear in the fan library.

Mic measurements are explicit and opt-in, processed locally by default. Unsupported routes may listen and receive Grooves but cannot claim verified speaker points. Paid music remains complete albums; nonmusic can sell complete shows/documentaries. No public-event authorization workflow or crowd map is hidden in the radio product.
