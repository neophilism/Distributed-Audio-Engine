# SceneSignal

SceneSignal is the community spatial-concert application, directly consuming Distributed Audio Engine. Free attendance, contributed speakers, artist lineups, spatial composition, whole-album commerce and sponsorship remain the product. Public session activation requires an encrypted permit upload or the organizer's specific no-permit-required attestation with site/conditions and an enforceable authorization lifetime.

Static manually placed speakers remain distinct from moving speakers. A speaker left on a blanket does not follow its owner's phone. Movement/tracking is accepted only when it follows the relevant physical object with supported confidence/calibration.

A **Spime** is a Bluetooth-enabled physical object, such as a statue, giant chess piece or literal violin, whose validated physical location represents the virtual location of a track/instrument group in a piece. A Spime can anchor the strings section while its audio is rendered by multiple nearby contributed speakers. It is not automatically a speaker or its owner's phone. BLE presence or raw signal strength never claims precise coordinates. Disconnected/stale tracking has a configured freeze/fade fallback and an operator can recover using manual placement.

Spime calibration, object identity, authorized controller, scene binding, tracking method and coordinate confidence are recorded. Private location/control telemetry and permits are endpoint encrypted. SceneSignal uses session contribution rewards independently of radio-specific point vesting and Unlocked Grooves.
