# Project instructions

Use End To End Everywhere as the security default for this and future development. Pin the upstream standard; map applicable profiles and evidence. Protected media, files, private manifests and private controls originate encrypted at authorized endpoints. Relays/storage never get their keys or plaintext. Never silently downgrade to TLS-only content protection. Do not roll your own key-establishment or group protocol. Authenticate before rendering; bound authorities, expiry, counters, device membership and retention. Keep intentional public disclosures and required business metadata explicitly inventoried.

Maintain four separate repositories and their boundaries. SceneSignal physical instrument anchors are called Spimes. Radio points, outro vesting and Unlocked Grooves belong to Distributed Radio Engine; consumer animation belongs to TrackZero.

Run the repository checks, update roadmap implementation/evidence and PR records, push sequential PRs, and merge passing PRs as authorized by the owner. Keep development merges distinct from production release: author self-review cannot supply independent E2EESA release evidence. Never turn simulated measurements or missing hardware reviews into passed field gates. Continue independent work around unresolved hardware or review gates.
